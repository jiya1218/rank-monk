Questions

Good questions, straight answers.

Clear answers about plans, billing, and switching anytime.

Can I upgrade or downgrade whenever I want?

Yes. Upgrade or downgrade at any time directly inside your account. Upgrades take effect immediately with prorated billing, and downgrades apply to your next renewal cycle.

Why are video generations billed separately?

Video rendering carries distinct computational GPU costs. To keep baseline subscriptions affordable (₹10,000/mo), you only pay for videos you actually render (~₹299–₹349 per 25-30 second video). All AI image generation and 2,000w+ articles are completely covered in your plan.

Do I have to connect my website?

To have Rank Monk auto-publish drafts directly to your CMS (WordPress, Webflow, Shopify, Ghost), yes. However, you can also manually copy markdown and HTML directly without connecting if your infrastructure is restricted.

What happens to unused blog article allowances?

Article quotas reset on each monthly billing cycle to maintain high generation quality and fresh topic research. If you need more articles in a month, you can easily top up or upgrade to Grow.

Are there any long-term contracts?

No. All subscriptions are strictly month-to-month unless you explicitly choose annual billing for the 20% discount. You can cancel at any time with a single click.
