Questions

Good questions, straight answers.

Clear answers about plans, billing, and switching anytime.

Can I upgrade or downgrade whenever I want?

Yes. Upgrade or downgrade at any time directly inside your account. Upgrades take effect immediately with prorated billing, and downgrades apply to your next renewal cycle.

How do the automatic audit and video improvement reports work?

Rank Monk continuously crawls your website, analyzes technical SEO issues, and synthesizes automated improvement reports. It also generates an actionable AI video walkthrough detailing exact code fixes, tag updates, and keyword opportunities you can implement with one click.

Is Rank Monk built for individual creators and solo businesses?

Yes. Every plan is crafted specifically for individual founders, solo creators, and growing businesses focusing on 1 website. You get an autonomous organic growth machine running crawls, keyword tracking, social scheduling, and content drafting for your brand without needing an agency retainer.

How does social media auto-scheduling work?

Whenever you publish an article or resolve an SEO milestone, Rank Monk automatically drafts and schedules engaging social posts across LinkedIn, X (Twitter), and other channels based on your predefined calendar and brand voice.

Are there any long-term contracts?

No. All subscriptions are strictly month-to-month unless you explicitly choose annual billing for the 20% discount. You can cancel at any time with a single click.
