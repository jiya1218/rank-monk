Knowledge & Answers

Good questions, straight answers.

Everything you need to know about autonomous article quality, CMS webhooks, AEO radar, and billing.

How does Rank Monk prevent generic AI sounding content?

During setup, Rank Monk crawls your website, top customer conversations, and past articles to build a strict Brand Voice Vector. Every generated piece is enriched with verified citations, original data tables, and custom diagrams, avoiding generic AI fluff.

What CMS platforms are supported for 1-click publishing?

We provide verified native webhook connectors for WordPress (REST API & Application Passwords), Webflow (CMS Collections), Shopify Blogs, and Ghost. Custom webhooks and Zapier/Make webhooks are supported on Grow and Scale plans.

What is Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) and how does it work?

Generative AI engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini) pull knowledge from authoritative web entities. Rank Monk scans these engines to check if your brand is named when prospective buyers ask relevant category questions, and creates targeted content to earn footnote citations.

How do the Self-Healing Technical SEO fixes work?

Our headless Chromium crawler scans your site for broken canonicals, missing meta tags, redirect loops, and schema errors. Instead of just exporting a CSV problem list, Rank Monk creates the exact patch code and pushes it via API or gives you a 1-click remedy.

Can I review articles before they go live on my website?

Yes. You can configure Rank Monk to publish as 'Draft' on your CMS so your editorial team can review before publishing, or set it to 'Autonomous Live' once you trust its output.

Can I upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time?

Yes. Subscriptions are month-to-month by default. You can change plans or cancel at any time directly in your account settings with zero cancellation fees.
