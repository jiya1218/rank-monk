Questions

Good questions, straight answers.

Everything you need to know about how Rank Monk operates.

Can I change or cancel my plan at any time?

Yes. Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel anytime directly from your settings. No long-term contracts, no cancellation penalties.

Why are video generations billed separately?

Video rendering carries distinct computational GPU costs. To keep baseline subscriptions accessible (₹10,000/mo), you only pay for videos you actually render (~₹299–₹349 per clip). Images and 2,500w articles are completely covered in your plan.

Do I have to connect my website CMS?

Connecting your CMS (WordPress, Webflow, Shopify, Ghost) allows 1-click automatic publishing. If you prefer to review manually, you can also copy finished articles and markdown code directly.

What is AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)?

AEO tracks how often your brand is cited as a factual footnote when prospective customers ask conversational questions in ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity, and tells you exactly what content to publish next.
