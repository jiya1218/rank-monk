Data Sovereignty

Privacy Policy.

Last updated: October 24, 2026. How we protect your brand data, search credentials, and AI memories.

1. Introduction & Core Guarantee

Rank Monk ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. Our core philosophy is simple: your workspace data and brand voice calibrations belong strictly to you. We do not sell your personal data, nor do we allow third parties to train public generative models on your private content.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide autonomous organic growth operations:

  • Account Credentials: Your name, work email address, and encrypted password.
  • Connected Domain Information: URLs, sitemaps, publicly accessible HTML headers (titles, meta tags, H1 elements), and schema tags extracted during technical crawlers.
  • Google Search Console Tokens: If you connect GSC, we store OAuth tokens securely via Supabase vault to fetch clicks, impressions, and query rankings.
  • Brand Identity Memory: Your calibrated brand voice sliders, target audience descriptions, and competitor lists.

3. AI Inference & Model Privacy Guarantee

Zero Training on Customer Data All AI generations executed through Rank Monk utilize enterprise-tier neural endpoints. Under our enterprise agreements, your inputs and generated outputs are never stored by model providers to train base models.

4. How We Use Information

We process domain information exclusively to calculate your technical SEO health score, diagnose broken tags, generate custom pillar articles matching your brand tone, track AEO citation probability on ChatGPT and Perplexity, and schedule social media broadcasts.

5. Data Security & Storage

Rank Monk employs bank-grade encryption: AES-256 for all stored data and credentials in Supabase PostgreSQL, and TLS 1.3 encryption for all data in transit. Daily automated backups are maintained with strict access controls.

6. Account Deletion & Right to be Forgotten

You maintain full sovereignty over your data. You may export all your articles, crawl records, and media assets at any time. If you choose to delete your account under Settings & Billing, all project data and connected domain records are permanently purged from our databases within 24 hours.

7. Compliance with Global Regulations

Rank Monk complies with the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP).
