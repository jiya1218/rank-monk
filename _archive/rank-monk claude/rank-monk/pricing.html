Questions

Good questions, straight answers.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Upgrade or downgrade at any time from your dashboard. Changes apply from your next billing date.

Why are videos billed separately?

Video generation costs more to produce than text or images, so we charge per clip. You only pay for the videos you actually make, roughly ₹299–₹349 each for a 25–30 second clip.

Do I need to connect my website?

To publish automatically, yes. You can also copy finished articles and paste them anywhere, and every plan includes the audit and AI search features.

What happens to unused blog articles?

Allowances reset each month. Unused articles do not roll over, but you can switch plans at any time if your needs change.

Is there a contract?

No. All plans are month-to-month and you can cancel from your account page at any time.
